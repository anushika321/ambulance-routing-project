Algorithm Analysis

Where each DAA concept is used in the project.

Dijkstra's Algorithm

Represents the city as a weighted graph and finds minimum road cost from an ambulance to the patient and from the patient to a hospital.

Matrix/simple implementation: O(V²)

Greedy Selection

After route costs are known, the system selects the locally best valid candidate: the available ambulance with minimum route cost and the valid hospital with minimum cost.

Candidate scan: O(A) / O(H)

Insertion Sort

The C engine ranks ambulance and hospital candidates by computed route cost. It is easy to demonstrate and is a standard comparison-based DAA sorting technique.

Worst case: O(n²)

Conflict / Constraint Checks

Before allocation, the engine rejects ambulances that are busy and hospitals that are full or closed, preventing invalid dispatches.

Space: O(V) for route state
